Zoetis | Manufacturing Marketing Intern

During my summer internship at Zoetis in Charles City, Iowa, I gained hands-on experience in marketing, communications, and community engagement. I helped plan and coordinate local events, including county fairs, National Night Out, and Zoetis’ “100 Years of Animal Health” celebration. My responsibilities included organizing event materials, helping with setup, creating promotional content, and interacting with community members.

I also created a variety of internal and external marketing materials using Canva, including employee announcements, benefit graphics, event promotions, and billboard designs. I helped promote events through local newspapers, radio, and Viva Engage by creating announcements and event recaps. In addition to my marketing responsibilities, I supported HR operations by helping correct timekeeping and employee record issues. This internship gave me the opportunity to work across different areas of the company while developing my communication, organization, and event coordination skills.

Campbell Street | Digital Media Intern

As a Digital Media Intern with Campbell Street Senior Living, I worked with the HR team to create marketing materials that supported recruitment and helped promote open positions. I designed hiring flyers, trifolds, standing posters, and other promotional materials to attract qualified applicants across different locations and roles.

A large part of my role involved managing multiple design requests at once and turning job information into clear, easy-to-understand materials. I worked closely with HR to make sure job details were accurate and that each piece followed the company’s branding. This experience strengthened my ability to stay organized, manage multiple projects, communicate with different teams, and create materials with a specific audience in mind.
